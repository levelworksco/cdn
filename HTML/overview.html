Overview

Most children begin developing the foundations of reading long before they are able to read independently. Through conversations, storytelling, songs, rhymes, and play, children build vocabulary, listening skills, and an understanding of language that support later reading development. The Reading Readiness Myth + 1

Early literacy experiences are often most effective when they are enjoyable, meaningful, and connected to a child's natural curiosity. Activities such as shared reading, storytelling, imaginative play, and exploration of sounds and symbols can help create positive associations with reading while strengthening foundational skills. The Reading Readiness Myth + 3
